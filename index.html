Keyboard Commands

    
Press Esc to dismiss · click anywhere to return to the command line

    

      

        
Movement

        
    
          
  • M — move forward
    • 
          
  • M B — move back
    • 
          
  • M L / M R — sidestep left / right
    • 
          
  • T L / T R / T A — turn left, right, around
    • 
          
  • C U / C D — climb up / down
    • 
        

      

      

        
Items

        
    
          
  • G R SW — get item (hand + class, e.g. sword)
    • 
          
  • P R SW — pull item from pack (or creature ahead)
    • 
          
  • D R — drop right-hand item
    • 
          
  • S L — stow left-hand item in pack
    • 
          
  • U R — use item (torch, flask, …)
    • 
          
  • A R — attack with right hand
    • 
        

      

      

        
Magic

        
    
          
  • R R — reveal ring or scroll
    • 
          
  • I FIRE / I ICE / I ENERGY / I FINAL — incant ring (right hand)
    • 
        

      

      

        
Info & game

        
    
          
  • E — examine ahead, floor, pack, and hands (E R for one hand)
    • 
          
  • L — look (reveal map)
    • 
          
  • Enter (empty) / F — flash your position on the map
    • 
          
  • ZS / ZL — save / load
    • 
          
  • THEME — choose visual theme
    • 
          
  • CRE / CREATURES — creature bestiary
    • 
        

      

      

        
Abbreviations

        
Hands: L left · R right

        
Classes: T torch · SW sword · SH shield · F flask · SC scroll · R ring
